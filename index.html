Welcome to Eagleland

Eagleland is a sunny, slightly strange country of seaside hills, dusty highways and glowing cities. Pack a bat, a bus ticket and a good appetite, because every stop has a story. This friendly guide helps you decide where to go first.

Why Visit?

Eagleland is a country built on the feeling of an American road trip. You can wander a friendly main street, cross a long stretch of desert and finish the day under the lights of a huge city. Nobody here is in a hurry, and everyone has a tip about where to eat next.
