Plan Your Visit

Getting Around

Eagleland is easy to explore, because the towns are linked by a friendly bus route and a few long, scenic highways. Keep these tips in mind before you set off.

  • Buses run between the towns all day, so keep a little spare change handy.
  • Carry snacks on the desert stretches, because services are far apart.
  • Check the weather before heading out, since evening storms roll in fast.
  • Ask a local for directions, because they love giving advice.
